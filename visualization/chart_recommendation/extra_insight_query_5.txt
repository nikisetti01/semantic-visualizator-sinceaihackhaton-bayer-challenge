PART 2: CHART RECOMMENDATIONS

**1. Chart Title:** Average Processing Time by Division  
   - **Chart Type:** Bar Chart  
   - **Visual Mapping:**  
     - X-Axis: division  
     - Y-Axis: avg_processing_time_days (Average)  
     - Color/Segment: division  
   - **Reasoning:**  
     This chart directly addresses the comparison of processing delays across divisions, highlighting which divisions experience longer average processing times. Bar charts are ideal for comparing values across discrete categories (divisions).

**2. Chart Title:** Total HSE Observations by Division  
   - **Chart Type:** Bar Chart  
   - **Visual Mapping:**  
     - X-Axis: division  
     - Y-Axis: total_observations (Sum or Count)  
     - Color/Segment: division  
   - **Reasoning:**  
     This chart allows for a clear comparison of incident/observation volume across divisions, helping to identify divisions with systematically higher numbers of observations.

**3. Chart Title:** Proportion of HSE Observations by Division  
   - **Chart Type:** Donut Chart  
   - **Visual Mapping:**  
     - X-Axis: N/A (Donut)  
     - Y-Axis: total_observations (Sum)  
     - Color/Segment: division  
   - **Reasoning:**  
     A donut chart visually communicates the share of total observations attributed to each division, supporting a part-to-whole analysis. This is appropriate since there are fewer than 5-7 divisions.
